# TradingBlox

### A Social Trading & Financial Market Simulation Platform

**TradingBlox** is a Roblox-based financial simulation game that combines stock-market trading, portfolio management, social trading, and investment-fund management into a unified financial ecosystem.

Inspired by professional trading platforms such as thinkorswim, accessible trading interfaces such as Robinhood, and the social discovery model of fomo.family, TradingBlox aims to transform traditional stock-market simulation into an interactive, competitive, and community-driven experience.

Players begin with **$50,000 in virtual capital** and progress from beginner investors to advanced traders, quantitative analysts, portfolio managers, and fund managers. They can trade traditional financial assets, analyze market data, follow other traders, publish investment theses, compete on performance leaderboards, and establish their own virtual investment firms.

Beyond traditional financial markets, TradingBlox introduces a proprietary **Roblox-native financial market** built around publicly available Roblox experience data. This allows players to research and speculate on the performance of virtual companies associated with Roblox experiences, creating an additional market ecosystem unique to the platform.

The ultimate goal is to bridge the gap between a financial simulator, a professional trading terminal, and a social investment network.

---

## 1. Core Concept

TradingBlox is built around five interconnected systems:

- **Trading:** Buy and sell stocks, ETFs, options, and other financial instruments as they become available through progression.
- **Research:** Analyze securities using fundamental data, technical indicators, financial news, and market analytics.
- **Social Trading:** Follow traders, discover investment opportunities, share market insights, and observe community trading activity.
- **Portfolio & Fund Management:** Build individual portfolios, collaborate with other players, and establish virtual investment firms.
- **Competition & Reputation:** Compete across multiple leaderboards based on profitability, consistency, analytical accuracy, and risk-adjusted performance.

These systems work together to create a persistent financial environment where players develop their own strategies, build reputations, and participate in an evolving market community.

## 2. Social Trading & Discovery

Inspired by fomo.family, TradingBlox integrates social activity directly into the trading experience rather than treating it as a separate feature.

### Social Trading Feed

The feed acts as a real-time stream of market activity across the TradingBlox ecosystem.

Players can discover:

- Recent trades and position updates shared by other traders.
- Popular stocks, ETFs, options, and Roblox-market securities.
- Investment theses, market commentary, and research publications.
- Trending assets and unusual trading activity.
- Top-performing traders and investment funds.
- Discussions surrounding market events and trading strategies.

Players can interact with posts, discuss investment ideas, and investigate assets directly from the feed.

Where appropriate, the platform can distinguish between actual executed trades, simulated trades, and user-published opinions so that social content remains informative.

### Trader Profiles

Every player can develop a public trading profile that functions as a financial résumé.

A profile may include:

- Portfolio value and historical performance.
- Total return and risk-adjusted return.
- Win rate, average gain/loss, and maximum drawdown.
- Trading activity and investment history.
- Published investment theses and research.
- Areas of expertise, such as technology, growth investing, options, or quantitative strategies.
- Memberships, roles, and accomplishments within investment funds.

Players can follow other traders to personalize their feeds and monitor their public activity.

### Following & Activity Notifications

Players can follow individual traders, investment funds, and potentially specific securities.

Notifications can alert users to new research publications, disclosed trades, fund announcements, and significant portfolio updates.

Following another player provides visibility into their activity; it does not automatically copy their trades unless a separate copy-trading feature is deliberately introduced.

### Asset Discovery

TradingBlox can use social signals to help players discover potentially interesting securities.

Potential discovery metrics include:

- Trading activity and volume.
- Number of traders watching an asset.
- Changes in community interest.
- Frequency of published investment theses.
- Recent price movements.
- Unusual market activity.
- Historical performance of traders discussing the asset.

Social popularity should be presented alongside fundamental and market data, rather than as a substitute for research.

## 3. Trading Terminal & Dashboard

TradingBlox combines professional-style financial analytics with a customizable interface designed for Roblox.

The dashboard serves as the player's financial command center, displaying account information, market conditions, portfolio performance, and social activity.

### Dashboard Modules

**Market Status**

- Market-open countdown and trading-session status.
- Pre-market, regular-hours, and after-hours information where supported.
- Upcoming economic events and earnings announcements.

**Portfolio Performance**

- Daily and cumulative profit and loss.
- Realized versus unrealized gains.
- Total account value and available cash.
- Buying power and margin utilization.
- Portfolio performance over time.

**Market Overview**

- Major market indices.
- Biggest gainers and losers.
- Trading volume and unusual activity.
- Sector performance and volatility.
- Trending assets across the TradingBlox community.

**Social Activity**

- Recent activity from followed traders.
- Trending investment theses.
- Top-performing traders and funds.
- New research publications.
- Relevant community discussions.

**Portfolio Allocation**

- Asset-allocation charts.
- Sector and individual-position exposure.
- Cash allocation and concentration metrics.

Players can customize their dashboards to suit their preferred playstyles, whether they focus on long-term investing, technical trading, options, quantitative strategies, or fund management.

## 4. Market Research & Trading

Each supported security has a dedicated research and trading page.

### Asset Overview

Depending on the instrument, players can access:

- Current price and daily change.
- Market capitalization and trading volume.
- Historical price data.
- Previous close and 52-week high/low.
- Earnings dates and dividend information.
- Relevant market news and announcements.

### Fundamental Analysis

Supported equities can provide financial metrics such as revenue, net income, earnings per share, valuation ratios, EBITDA, free cash flow, debt, profit margins, and growth rates.

### Technical Analysis

Charts can support indicators and tools such as:

- RSI and MACD.
- Simple and exponential moving averages.
- VWAP.
- Bollinger Bands and ATR.
- Volume Profile.
- Support and resistance.
- Momentum analysis and drawing tools.

### Order Execution

Players can progressively unlock additional order types, including market, limit, stop, stop-limit, bracket, and trailing-stop orders.

The trading interface can include compact Active Trader windows, allowing players to submit orders without leaving the chart.

Advanced progression may unlock options chains, Greeks, margin, portfolio hedging, and quantitative analytics.

## 5. Portfolio Management & Analytics

TradingBlox encourages players to evaluate their performance beyond the amount of virtual money they accumulate.

Each position can display its ticker, quantity, average entry price, current price, market value, unrealized profit or loss, percentage return, daily change, and portfolio weight.

Portfolio-level analytics may include:

- Total and daily returns.
- Win rate and average gain/loss.
- Maximum drawdown.
- Volatility and Sharpe ratio.
- Portfolio beta.
- Sector allocation and concentration.
- Leverage and risk exposure.
- Options exposure and margin utilization.

This creates a more meaningful performance system in which players must consider both returns and the risks taken to achieve them.

## 6. Roblox-Native Financial Market

One of TradingBlox's defining features is its integration of publicly available Roblox experience data into a separate virtual market.

Experiences can be represented by simulated securities associated with virtual companies or projects. Their prices and valuations can respond to relevant data and a defined simulation model.

Potential data inputs include:

- Concurrent player counts.
- Visits and engagement.
- Player growth and retention, where available.
- Likes and other public engagement indicators.
- Update frequency and developer activity.
- Trending status.
- Revenue estimates, where reliable data is available.

Players can research these virtual securities, identify emerging trends, and develop investment theses around the growth and popularity of Roblox experiences.

This creates a market environment that extends beyond traditional stocks and introduces an economy connected directly to the Roblox ecosystem.

The simulation model should clearly distinguish observable metrics from estimates and simulated prices. Market prices should not be presented as verified real-world valuations of Roblox developers or experiences.

## 7. Player-Created Investment Funds

TradingBlox allows players to establish and manage virtual investment firms, transforming individual trading into a collaborative experience.

Fund creators can:

- Establish a fund name, profile, and investment strategy.
- Recruit members and assign organizational roles.
- Build and manage a shared portfolio.
- Publish research and investment theses.
- Track performance and risk metrics.
- Establish internal investment rules.
- Build a reputation within the TradingBlox ecosystem.

### Example Fund Roles

**Fund Manager:** Oversees the organization and its overall investment strategy.

**Portfolio Manager:** Manages a designated portfolio or investment mandate.

**Research Analyst:** Investigates securities and produces investment theses.

**Quantitative Analyst:** Develops data-driven strategies and quantitative models.

**Risk Manager:** Monitors portfolio exposure, volatility, and drawdowns.

**Trader:** Executes approved investment decisions.

Funds can compete against one another through dedicated leaderboards, research challenges, and portfolio-performance competitions.

This system turns TradingBlox into a platform where players can build virtual investment organizations rather than simply accumulate individual wealth.

## 8. Progression & Unlockable Capabilities

Players begin with basic financial tools and unlock more advanced capabilities as they gain experience.

**Level 1 — Beginner**

- Stocks and ETFs.
- Basic orders and portfolio tracking.
- Simple charts.

**Level 2 — Investor**

- Limit orders and short selling.
- Fundamental analysis and earnings data.
- Advanced chart indicators and portfolio analytics.

**Level 3 — Advanced Trader**

- Options and options chains.
- Greeks and advanced technical indicators.
- Margin and short strategies.

**Level 4 — Professional**

- Advanced options strategies and portfolio hedging.
- Advanced order types.
- Algorithmic tools and quantitative analytics.
- Investment-fund management.

Progression should provide more sophisticated tools and responsibilities rather than simply granting more capital. Social features, research publication, and community participation can develop alongside trading progression.

## 9. Competition, Reputation & Leaderboards

TradingBlox supports multiple competitive categories to reward different approaches to investing.

**Highest Portfolio Value:** Rewards accumulated virtual wealth.

**Highest Return:** Measures percentage gains over a defined period.

**Best Risk-Adjusted Return:** Rewards returns relative to risk.

**Best Fund:** Compares eligible player-created investment funds.

**Best Trader:** Evaluates a broader set of trading-performance metrics.

**Best Analyst:** Rewards the accuracy and quality of published investment theses.

**Consistency:** Highlights players who demonstrate sustained performance over time.

Leaderboards should account for factors such as sample size, evaluation period, drawdowns, and risk exposure where relevant. This reduces the emphasis on isolated wins and encourages more thoughtful competition.

## 10. Overall Vision

TradingBlox is designed to combine the analytical depth of a trading terminal, the accessibility of a financial simulator, and the community dynamics of a social trading platform.

Players can discover markets, research securities, execute simulated trades, build portfolios, follow other traders, publish investment theses, create funds, and compete across multiple dimensions of financial performance.

Its Roblox-native market adds a distinct layer to the experience by allowing players to explore a simulated economy built around Roblox experiences themselves.

The long-term vision is to create a persistent, Roblox-native financial ecosystem in which players can develop their skills, establish reputations, collaborate through virtual investment firms, and experience the interconnected roles of traders, investors, analysts, and portfolio managers.

**TradingBlox: Discover markets. Develop strategies. Build your portfolio. Establish your fund.**
